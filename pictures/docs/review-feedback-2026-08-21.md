## Design synthesis

1. Use **A1** as the master identity concept because it is memorable and directly
   communicates book, writing, portal, world, and Q in one mark.
2. Do not force the detailed A1 artwork into favicon scale. Derive the final
   compact icon from the **top-center icon-study treatment**, retaining A1's
   doorway/portal meaning.
3. Replace rather than polish A banner 1. A future version should use organic
   page curves and A1's doorway language, avoiding rigid block architecture.
4. Simplify A banner 2 around one immediate visual sentence: one book unfolds
   into two or three clearly separated worlds. Reduce ornaments, tiny landmarks,
   and competing focal points.
5. Regenerate B from the **V1 form reference** with genuine alpha, preserving its
   circular book silhouette and indirect omega impression. The current clean B
   logo remains a practical fallback.
6. Preserve C banner's parchment, bookplate, and archival richness as a reusable
   campaign/chapter/collection style reference rather than automatically making
   it the universal core banner.
