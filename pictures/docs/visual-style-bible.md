# Quillverse Visual Style Bible — Gate 1

## Master brand

Quillverse is a universal living-world storytelling platform. The master brand
must work across historical, fantasy, mystery, school, nature, and ocean worlds.
World-pack motifs belong to their packs and do not enter the core mark.

## Primary direction: A — Living Manuscript

Approved as the primary direction for exploration:

- abstract quill plus open book or narrative portal;
- optional subtle negative-space Q;
- deep ink blue, warm ivory, parchment, and restrained muted gold;
- literary, calm, premium, modern enough for software;
- simple vector-friendly silhouette that survives favicon scale;
- no Regency-only decoration, crown, heraldry, stars, or floral motifs.

## Comparison direction: B — Celestial Archive

Comparison only. Uses restrained constellation language to show a more overtly
multiversal/magical alternative. It must remain elegant, not become a galaxy or
fantasy-game logo.

## Comparison direction: C — Storybook Crest

Comparison only. Uses a bookplate/crest structure to show a more archival and
prestigious alternative. It must avoid royalty, family arms, university-badge
styling, and Regency-specific heraldry.

## Gate-1 status

All ten planned assets are `needs-review`. No concept is approved for app use,
and no production icon, favicon, banner, manifest, Angular file, D1/R2 record,
or deployed asset may be replaced during this gate.
