# Brand assets

Quillverse identity, banner, and presentation artwork.

## Folders

- `final/logos/`: cleaned transparent color and monochrome master marks.
- `final/wordmarks/`: horizontal color and monochrome Quillverse wordmarks.
- `final/icons/`: transparent, light, and dark PNG families at
  16/32/48/192/512 pixels.
- `concepts/living-manuscript/`: the selected direction's reviewed logo, icon,
  tile, and banner studies. `preferred/` marks the user's favorite within a
  comparison group; `alternatives/` contains positively reviewed backups.
- `concepts/celestial-archive/` and `concepts/storybook-crest/`: useful B/C
  comparison directions retained for future worlds or campaigns.
- `presentation/`: approved covers, poster, and wide README/GitHub hero.

`final/` means the image derivative itself is technically complete. Nothing in
this folder has been wired into the application; that remains a separate task.
Rejected or technically invalid brand work is under `../archive/`.
