# Universal visual templates

Reusable, world-neutral composition and style references for Quillverse.

## Folders

- `characters/`: identity-consistent portrait, half-body, and expression-sheet
  templates.
- `locations/`: exterior, interior, street, landscape, and landmark/event-space
  composition references.
- `maps/`: preferred pin-safe atlas and painterly bases plus three greener
  manuscript variants in `manuscript-variants/`.
- `ornaments/living-manuscript/`: primary decorative UI direction.
- `ornaments/historical-neutral/`: quieter realistic parchment/ink alternative.
- `placeholders/`: unknown-character and unknown-location artwork.
- `story-scenes/`: neutral dialogue-safe narrative stage.
- `textures/`: world-neutral archival paper and ink texture.

These files demonstrate reusable framing and style; future worlds do not need
to reuse the literal images. Brand marks live separately under `../brand/`.
Generation and validation details are indexed in `../docs/README.md`.
